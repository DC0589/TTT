STAGE 3 · AI-POWERED PRACTICE

Mock interviews powered by AI

Our mock interviews use AI to create fresh questions for every session and to give feedback on each answer, so students practise like they will be tested in a real interview.

How the AI mock works

  • Ten questions per session, matched to the role and level (easy, medium or hard) the student picks.
  • Spoken and coding questions. Coding questions test real problem solving such as loops, strings, lists and SQL, not memorised syntax.
  • Based on real interviews. Questions draw on the kinds of questions asked in actual company interviews.
  • Your own topics. Students can focus a session on topics such as looping or data types.
  • No repeats. Questions a student has already seen are not asked again.
  • Scored 1 to 5 with feedback on correctness, completeness, clarity and, for code, edge cases and efficiency.

AI feedback is a practice aid to help students improve before real interviews.
